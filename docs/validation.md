Generated by `python -m engine.calibrate`. Models are fitted on 360 simulated matches (seasons 1 to 4) and scored on 90 matches they never saw (season 5).

## Game-state effects on scoring, learned from minute-by-minute xG

| Situation | Hand-set before | Learned |
|---|---|---|
| Down a man (per player) | 0.72x | 0.78x |
| Up a man (per player) | 1.25x | 1.14x |
| Losing | 1.00x | 1.09x |
| Winning | 1.00x | 0.95x |
| After the 75th minute | 1.00x | 1.01x |

Goals still to come: a Negative Binomial with size 20 fits better than Poisson, so the forecast uses it.

## Shape detection against the block each team really played

12,757 samples, one every 5 seconds a team was out of possession, from 12 held-out matches with full tracking (half with a red card). Agreement: 86.2%.

| True block | Detected high press | Detected mid block | Detected low block | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High press | 1,914 | 732 | 0 | 72% |
| Mid block | 223 | 7,316 | 154 | 95% |
| Low block | 0 | 654 | 1,764 | 73% |

Precision: High press 90%, Mid block 84%, Low block 92%.
Detection waits 60 seconds before confirming a change, so some disagreement right after a switch is expected.
