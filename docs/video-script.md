# Demo video script: "The Gameday XI"

Target length: 1:55. Spoken words: about 265 (a calm keynote pace, 2.3 words a second).
Style: black title slides with one line of white text, then the product. Short pauses. Let the 3D do the talking.
Rules: synthetic data only on screen, no real club names, logos or trademarks, no copyrighted music.

## The team (use these names on screen and in the README)

Eleven AI agents on Microsoft Foundry, one squad. Code is the referee.

| No. | Nickname | Agent | What it does on the pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Safe Hands | Analyst | Catches every question in Ask the match through 15 MCP tools. Never lets in a made-up number. |
| 2 | The Sweeper | Summarizer | Catch me up: three points for a fan who just arrived. |
| 3 | The Scout | Preview | Form, head to head and players to watch before kick-off. |
| 4 | The Debrief | Post-game | What decided the match, and what each club should watch next. |
| 5 | The Interpreter | Personalizer | Every verified line in Spanish and Hindi. |
| 6 | The Anchor | In-depth Analyst | Reads the game every 15 minutes: why it is going this way. |
| 7 | The Wingers | Narrator (casual and analyst) | Two Narrators run in parallel down both flanks for every key moment. |
| 8 | The Voice | Commentator | The same moment in Classic, Hype, Stats or Tactician, spoken by Azure AI Speech. |
| 9 | The Finisher | Explainer | Explain this moment: how every goal was made, beat by beat, in 3D. |
| 10 | The Playmaker | Recap | The match report on the Blueprint. |
| 11 | The Back Page | Newsdesk | A newspaper report for every fixture of the matchday. |
| 12 | The Super-Sub | Producer | The newest signing: picks the clips and presents the Highlights reel. |
| Ref | VAR | Verifier (code, not AI) | Reviews every line. Numbers and names are filled by code; any claim the data cannot back is sent back. |

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1920x1080, browser zoom 100%, full screen (F11), notifications off (Focus assist on).
- Before recording: open the live app, set speed so a goal has happened, open Match Theatre once so the 3D is warm.
- Record each row as its own short take. Re-record a take instead of fixing it in the edit.
- Voice: record the voiceover separately in a quiet room, then lay it over the takes. Read slowly; pause on the black slides.
- No music, or royalty-free music you have rights to. No real Premier League names, crests or footage anywhere.
